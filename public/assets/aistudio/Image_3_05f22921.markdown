## Brand & Style

The design system establishes an elevated architectural aesthetic tailored for high-end interior spaces, smart finishings, and digital design services. Balancing tactile craftsmanship with fluid commerce, the visual language merges modern minimalism with refined physical product cards.

Designed primarily for mobile Android experiences within an active East African market, the interface projects precision, reliability, and bespoke luxury. High-contrast structure, crisp hairline boundaries, and restrained scarlet accents build immediate trust for large-ticket architectural finishes, decor purchases, and professional interior consultations.

## Colors

The palette centers on a disciplined crimson tone (`#A81825`) as the primary brand signature, representing craft, passion, and authority. Crisp architectural neutrals anchor the layout: pure canvas white (`#FFFFFF`) with staggered off-white foundational surfaces (`#F8F9FA` and `#F5F5F5`), bound by low-contrast borders (`#E5E7EB`). Charcoal text (`#202020`) replaces harsh absolute black to maintain warmth and editorial readability.

Regional commerce and communication triggers integrate distinct functional accents:
- **Primary Brand:** Deep Crimson (`#A81825`) applied to primary call-to-actions, active tab indicators, and essential navigation highlights.
- **Transactional Accent:** M-Pesa Green (`#00A859`) reserved specifically for payment prompts, balance verification, and checkout completion milestones.
- **Direct Support Channel:** WhatsApp Emerald (`#25D366`) designated strictly for immediate design consultation links, direct contractor messaging, and real-time support triggers.
- **Surface Scale:** Surface Base (`#FFFFFF`), Surface Alt (`#F8F9FA`), and Surface Raised (`#F5F5F5`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances architectural geometric presence with pragmatic mobile readability:
- **Headlines (Outfit):** Delivers clean, contemporary proportions evoking modern spatial design and showroom clarity. Display weights use tight letter-spacing to command immediate visual focus without excessive visual bulk.
- **Body & Controls (DM Sans):** Selected for its neutral, highly legible rhythm across small-format handheld screens. Maintains consistent legibility across long interior specifications, bill of quantities, and order details.
- **Currency & Numerics:** Product prices and quotation figures use `price-display` (Outfit Semibold/Bold) prepended with standard regional currency formatting (`KSh 12,500`), keeping monetary metrics clear and prominent.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt-derived rhythm optimized for 360dp–412dp Android viewports. A 4-column fluid layout with `16px` (`1rem`) gutters and outer margins forms the structural foundation.

Key spatial rules:
- **Touch Boundaries:** All interactive components (navigation items, quantity steppers, action buttons) strictly enforce a minimum touch target size of 48×48dp, even when the visible glyph or badge is smaller.
- **Vertical Spacing:** Modules adhere strictly to `space-md` (16px) for interior card grouping, `space-lg` (24px) between distinct contextual sections, and `space-xl` (32px) for page transitions and primary headline anchors.
- **Sticky Screen Safe Areas:** Mobile templates account for a 64dp top application bar and an 80dp bottom navigation bar, with content pads cleared to prevent scrolling overlap.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages low-contrast structural boundaries paired with soft, highly diffused ambient drop shadows rather than heavy skeuomorphic layers.

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Pure background surfaces (`#FFFFFF` or `#F8F9FA`) with no elevation.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Product Tiles):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a 1px hairline border (`#E5E7EB`) and an ambient drop shadow: `0px 2px 6px rgba(32, 32, 32, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns & Floating Actions):** Raised tactile elements using `0px 4px 16px rgba(32, 32, 32, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Sheets & Sticky Navigation):** Grounded floating layers using `0px -2px 12px rgba(32, 32, 32, 0.06)`, providing clear separation from underlying dynamic catalog feeds without overwhelming the primary content.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a soft, architectural corner treatment (`roundedness: 1`), instilling an aesthetic of machined precision and fine interior cabinetry:
- **Standard Controls & Text Fields:** 4px (`0.25rem`) corner radius for inputs, text containers, and segmented control chips.
- **Cards & Visual Containers:** 8px (`0.5rem`, `rounded-lg`) corner radius for catalog tiles, service quotation modules, and bottom sheet containers.
- **Status Badges & Category Pills:** 12px (`0.75rem`, `rounded-xl`) or fully circular pills for tags, product availability indicators, and counter badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Solid deep crimson (`#A81825`) with pure white text, 48dp minimum height, 4px border radius. Used for booking consultations, adding items to cart, and progressing checkout.
- **Secondary / M-Pesa Checkout Button:** Solid brand green (`#00A859`) with white text, exclusively used for the final "Pay via M-Pesa" action.
- **WhatsApp Direct Action:** Compact or full-width button featuring a subtle background tint (`#E8FBF0`), green text (`#25D366`), and the WhatsApp icon for immediate design advice.
- **Outlined Button:** Transparent fill, 1px border (`#E5E7EB`), text `#202020`. Used for secondary actions (e.g., "View Specifications").

### Sticky Bottom Navigation (5 Tabs)
- Fixed 80dp dock with subtle top border (`#E5E7EB`) and elevation level 3.
- Five equidistant tabs: **Home**, **Shop**, **Cart** (with pill counter badge in `#A81825`), **Services**, and **Account**.
- Inactive state: `#6B7280` icon and label. Active state: `#A81825` with filled icon variant and bold label.

### Cards (Product & Service)
- Built on `#FFFFFF` with 8px radius and a 1px border in `#E5E7EB`.
- Product images use a constrained 1:1 or 4:3 aspect ratio with an off-white background (`#F8F9FA`).
- Typography grouping inside the card: category badge, product title, and bold price token in Outfit (`KSh XX,XXX`).

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Horizontally scrollable filter rails with 48dp tap zones.
- Unselected: Surface `#F5F5F5`, text `#202020`.
- Selected: Background `#A81825`, text `#FFFFFF`.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Height 48dp, 1px border (`#E5E7EB`), focus state shifts to a 1.5px `#A81825` border with zero outline ring.
- Top-aligned mini labels for field clarity during typing.
